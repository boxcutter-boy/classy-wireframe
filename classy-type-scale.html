Classy × Material 3 — the Type Scale

Material 3 collapses type into five roles — Display, Headline, Title, Body, Label — and is built to run on two typefaces: a brand face and a plain face. Classy already works this way.

Plain typeface → ITC Franklin Gothic

Title · Body · Label
⚠

Classy's one deviation: the brand face reaches one role deeper — into Title Large — so section headers ("Appearance & accessibility") and card / person names ("Riley") stay Clarendon. Title Medium & Small drop back to Franklin Gothic for genuinely utilitarian UI. Clarendon never appears at Body or Label size — it's too heavy a slab to read small, which is exactly why it stops here.

The one place the scale is overridden

On a personal ad, the member picks their own fonts — but only in two slots: the ad headline (a Headline slot) and the ad body (a Body slot). Those are the decorative faces in the feed. Every other surface in the app stays locked to this scale — which is also what the "Hide user-selected fonts" accessibility toggle enforces by snapping ads back onto the plain face.

This sheet references the real Adobe faces by name, so it renders in Clarendon URW & ITC Franklin Gothic wherever they're activated; elsewhere it falls back to a slab serif and a gothic sans. Sizes shown are M3's baseline (Roboto-reference) scale — keep them, or scale as a set.
